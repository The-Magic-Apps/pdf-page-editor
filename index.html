PDF Page Editor

Merge and split PDFs, and reorder, rotate, flip and remove pages, all in Microsoft Edge. Nothing is uploaded: your files never leave your computer.

Merge and split

Combine any number of PDFs, or take just the pages you need: drag them, select them, or type a range such as 3-7, 12.

Arrange pages

Reorder by dragging, rotate left or right, flip, and remove pages. Undo and redo anything.

See before you save

Preview any page large, exactly as it will be saved, then save one new PDF where you choose.

Private by design

No uploads, no accounts, no tracking. The extension asks for no permissions and makes no network requests.
