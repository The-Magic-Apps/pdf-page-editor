Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Last updated: 18 November 2026

PDF Page Editor is an extension for Microsoft Edge that combines, splits and rearranges the pages of PDF files. This policy describes what it does with your files and what, if anything, leaves your computer.

What we collect

Nothing. PDF Page Editor does not run any analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising or third-party tracking code. There are no user accounts, and we do not operate any servers that receive or store your data.

Your files

  • They stay on your computer. PDFs you open are read and processed entirely inside your browser. They are never uploaded anywhere: the extension makes no network requests at all.
  • They are held only while the editor is open. Opened files are kept in the browser tab's memory and are discarded when you close the tab. Nothing about them is written to disk except the PDF you choose to save, in the place you choose.
  • Passwords are not stored. If you open a password-protected PDF, the password you type is kept in memory only while that file is open, and is never saved or sent anywhere.

What is stored in your browser

Two layout preferences are saved in the browser's storage for this extension: the position of the divider between the panes, and the page size you choose in each pane. They contain no information about your files. Removing the extension removes them.

Permissions

The extension asks for no browser permissions. It cannot read the websites you visit, your browsing history or your other tabs.

Children

The extension collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
